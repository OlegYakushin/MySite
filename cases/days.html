Travel planning · iOS

Days travel tracker.

An iOS app for travel history, upcoming trips, country totals, visa records and recurring travel planning workflows.

SwiftUIWidgetsTravel DataApp Store

Problem

Frequent travelers need trip records, country totals and visa context in one place without spreadsheet maintenance.

Solution

Built an offline trip history, stay-day calendar, Schengen 90/180 planning and document records, with optional forecasts and iCloud sync.

Outcome

Available on the App Store with an offline core tracker and optional Premium forecasts, reminders and iCloud sync.

Engineering Highlights

  • SwiftUI navigation across travel, visa, home and settings surfaces.
  • Trip state split into all, past and upcoming records.
  • Reusable visual system for empty states, add flows and country/visa data.
