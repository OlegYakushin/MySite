Before your next trip.

Is DAY’S free?

The app is free to download. Trip logging, document records and the current stay-day counter are free. Premium is an optional monthly subscription.

Does it track my location?

No. You enter countries and dates manually. DAY’S does not require GPS or a separate app account.

Does it work offline?

The core tracker and locally saved records work offline. Purchases and iCloud synchronization need a connection.

Can I plan a future stay?

Yes. Future stay forecasts are a Premium feature and use your recorded travel history and configured rule.

Does a day count guarantee entry?

No. Results depend on your entered dates, documents and history coverage. DAY’S does not confirm entry or work permission. Check the rules that apply to your circumstances with official authorities.

Which languages are available?

The public version 2.0 is available in English, Spanish and Russian.
