DAY’S for iPhone

An offline travel log and stay-day tracker for people who make repeated trips between countries.

DAY’S brings manually recorded travel dates, a stay-day calendar and passport or visa details together. The free core works offline without a DAY’S account. An optional monthly Premium subscription adds future stay forecasts, local limit reminders and private iCloud sync.

Developer: Oleg Yakushin

Public version: 2.0, released 13 October 2026

Languages: English, Spanish and Russian

Price: Free download and core tracker. Optional monthly Premium; the Spanish App Store lists Premium at €0.99. Regional pricing is shown before purchase.

App Store: DAY’S on the App Store

Contact: hello@olegotka.es

What changed in 2.0

A rebuilt travel and business-trip workflow, a guided stay-day setup, document-date checks, local backups and clearer explanations when travel history is incomplete. Premium includes future forecasts, reminders and iCloud synchronization.

DAY’S is a planning tool. Calculations depend on the history and documents entered; the app does not confirm entry or work permission.
