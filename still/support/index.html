A little help with Still

Still is currently being built. App Store availability and a download link will be announced on the main page after release.

Contact

Email hello@olegotka.es with “Still” in the subject. English, Spanish and Russian are welcome. Include the app version, device/OS and the steps that led to the problem. Do not send passwords, payment-card information, detailed medical records or your full journal.

No measurements yet?

Check whether the measurements exist in Apple Health and whether the app can read the relevant types. Wear the Watch as needed for those measurements. At least fourteen distinct days of HRV and resting heart rate are required for the experimental personal comparison; existing usable Health history can count. Missing data remains missing. The clearly labeled demo lets you explore separately.

Plus does not appear

Use Restore purchases in Settings with the purchasing Apple account. Check subscriptions in Apple account settings and allow pending approvals to finish. The yearly trial is three days for eligible customers only; monthly has no trial. The website itself does not sell a subscription.

Reflection availability

Apple AI depends on the device, system version, language and settings. An unavailable model or unsupported language uses ready-made questions. Russian currently uses this editorial experience. Your original note is preserved when generation fails.

Notifications and social cards

Enable selected reminders in Still, then approve notifications in the system prompt. Quiet hours and completed check-ins affect scheduling. For social cards, review the image, choose the format and explicitly enable any measurements. The system share menu requires you to choose and complete the sending action. Photos saving uses an add-only permission.

Privacy and deletion

Export or delete your entries in Settings. Deleting Still entries does not delete original Apple Health measurements. Send privacy requests to the contact above; see our privacy policy.
