Before you join

Can it wake or unlock my Mac?

No. Your Mac must be awake, online and running the agent. Unlocking macOS, Touch ID and macOS password prompts require you at the computer.

Is screen viewing a live video stream?

It uses requested still images without audio, with iCloud delivery latency. Screen viewing and control stop when the Mac is locked or asleep.

What do I need?

iOS 17 or later and macOS 14 or later, using the same iCloud account. The Mac agent supports Apple silicon and Intel. Widget refresh timing is controlled by the system.

What happens to cleanup data?

You review the exact selection before selected data is permanently deleted or apps are asked to quit normally. Documents, Photos, Downloads, Xcode archives and simulator device data are excluded.
