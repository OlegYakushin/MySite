Purpose and operator

Still is operated by Oleg Yakushin, Barcelona, Spain. Contact hello@olegotka.es. Still is intended for adults seeking general well-being and recovery context.

A personal comparison, not medical advice

Still compares available physiological measurements with your personal history and offers self-reported check-ins, journaling and breathing pauses. Its index is experimental. It is not a diagnosis, disease prediction, clinical recovery certification, emergency service or continuous measurement of emotional stress. Missing data or a calm-looking score does not exclude illness. Use your own judgment and appropriate professional care when you feel unwell.

Recorded workout duration is a factual summary of available records, not a medically validated training load or permission to intensify exercise. Breathing guidance is optional: breathe comfortably and stop if uncomfortable.

Availability and data

Apple Watch model, region, Health permissions, wear time and operating system affect available data. Background updates, widgets and notifications follow system scheduling and may be delayed. Journal and breathing work without all optional measurements. Clearly labeled demo data is synthetic and kept separate from your actual records.

Apple AI reflection requires a compatible device, supported language, enabled settings and a ready model. Ready-made questions remain available. AI can be wrong or refuse a request; verify the result yourself. No external AI API is promised in the first version.
