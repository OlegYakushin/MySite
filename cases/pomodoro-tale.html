The product

Keep the timer, scene and sound controls easy to reach while reducing distractions during a focus session.

Apple platforms

SwiftUI interfaces adapt the focus experience to iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. Widgets provide a small companion surface outside the main app.

Session continuity

Onboarding remembers the selected duration and sound. Existing sessions bypass first-run setup, while active-session settings protect the current interval.

Engineering highlights

  • Configurable focus and break intervals with a round-by-round session flow.
  • Scene selection and separate music and ambience controls.
  • Optional onboarding with a free path, plus accessibility and large-text layouts.
