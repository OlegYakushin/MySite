Your Mac. Your data.

Updated 28 October 2026.

Device data and commands

MacReport uses the private CloudKit database in your Apple iCloud account to connect your devices. It stores system readings, a seven-day sampled history, the latest command and response, requested app lists, and cleanup plans and results. The developer does not run a separate telemetry server or use an advertising or analytics SDK in the apps.

Screen viewing

Screen viewing is off by default and requires permission and a local toggle on your Mac. Requested images are uploaded to your private iCloud, without audio. The iPhone accepts the requested frame for two minutes and discards it when the viewer closes or backgrounds. The running, online Mac agent removes the last cloud image on expiry, disable or lock. This is not a server-enforced deletion timer; an offline or closed agent cannot perform that deletion.

Direct Live screen

When you choose Live, your devices exchange short-lived connection details through your private iCloud. A WebRTC connection then carries screen frames and control messages directly between the Mac and iPhone, protected by DTLS encryption, without audio or recording. Live frames and input are not stored in CloudKit or on a developer-operated relay. Google public STUN servers receive network connection information to help the devices find each other; they do not receive screen contents. Connection details can contain network addresses and remain in your private iCloud until replaced.

Live requires the same local Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions, screen-sharing and control switches. Closing or backgrounding the iPhone viewer closes its connection and clears its image. The Mac stops when it locks, sleeps, permissions are disabled, the input session expires or viewer heartbeats stop. Some networks cannot establish a direct connection; this version offers requested iCloud images as a separate fallback and does not configure a TURN relay.

Opening apps and screen control

The agent can report installed application names and bundle identifiers through your private iCloud and open an application chosen from that report. Screen control requires a separate local opt-in, Screen Recording and Accessibility permission on the Mac. In the requested-image mode, clicks, scroll coordinates, fixed keys and execution results use the private command slot. In Live they use the direct encrypted connection. Sessions expire after two idle minutes, with a fifteen-minute maximum; a locked or sleeping Mac does not accept input.

Text is encrypted for the Mac before upload using system cryptography. The device-local private key stays in the Mac login Keychain; the iPhone obtains its public key through your private iCloud. Typed text is not stored in telemetry diagnostics or history and does not use the Mac clipboard. The encrypted text envelope is removed from execution and final acknowledgements. An undelivered encrypted command may remain in the cloud slot until replacement or agent processing. The entry sheet clears its local buffer on send, cancel or background. Text visible inside the controlled application can appear in requested screen images.

Cleanup

Analysis runs locally on the Mac. It inspects known cache and diagnostic-report locations, file metadata, open-file activity and app resource readings. Names, paths and recommendations are sent to your own devices through private iCloud. File contents are not uploaded for cleanup analysis. The app asks you to review exact items before permanently deleting selected data or requesting normal app termination. Personal document and photo directories are excluded.

Notifications and local storage

CloudKit and Apple Push Notification service deliver configured event alerts. Apple Watch notification delivery follows your device settings. Readings and downloaded history are cached locally; the Mac keeps local telemetry diagnostics and history. An optional stale-data reminder is scheduled locally and does not establish that the Mac is offline.

Updates and this website

The Mac agent checks this website for updates when you enable checks or request one. The web host may receive the network address and ordinary request information needed to serve a page or download. This product page has no analytics script or advertising tracker. If you join the early-access waitlist, the separate waitlist service described below receives your email. Sparkle update archives must be authenticated with the application's update-signing key.

Early-access waitlist

If you submit the form, Oleg Yakushin stores your email address, signup date and the version of your consent to receive a MacReport invitation and early-access updates. This list is separate from app data in your private iCloud. Joining the list does not give the developer access to your Mac, iCloud or screen.

The waitlist service is hosted through OpenAI Sites with Cloudflare D1 storage. These providers also process ordinary request information needed to deliver and secure the service. Subscriber addresses are available only to the owner after sign-in and can be exported privately to send invitations. This form does not send an automatic email or invitation.

We use the address for MacReport invitations and early-access updates, not advertising for other products. You can withdraw your consent or ask to have your address removed by emailing hello@olegotka.es. Addresses are retained while the early-access list is in use or until you ask for removal.

Your choices

You can disable remote control, screen sharing, notifications and automatic update checks in the apps or system settings. Quitting the Mac agent stops new collection and command processing. Apple manages the iCloud and CloudKit infrastructure under your account settings. For questions, contact the developer through olegotka.es.
