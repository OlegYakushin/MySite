Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. The iPhone app is currently in private TestFlight testing.

Know what is happening

Battery, CPU, RAM, free disk space and seven days of sampled history. iPhone widgets and Apple Watch complications keep the last reading close.

Make room thoughtfully

Review old build caches, update installers and crash reports. Choose heavy apps to close. Every action shows its purpose and requires confirmation.

See and control

Open installed apps such as ChatGPT or Chrome. Tap the Mac image, scroll and send encrypted text or keys. Control needs separate permission and an unlocked, awake Mac.

Keep work moving

Adjust brightness, lock the Mac or set a keep-awake timer. Use fixed scenarios, Siri shortcuts and notifications for important changes.

Set up your Mac

  1. Open the disk image and drag MacReport Agent into Applications.
  2. Open the agent. Use the same iCloud account on the Mac and iPhone, and keep the agent running.
  3. Enable launch at login if wanted. For screen viewing, grant Screen & System Audio Recording permission and enable sharing in the agent.
  4. Open MacReport on the iPhone and choose your Mac.

A sleeping or offline Mac cannot receive commands. Screen viewing and control use requested still images, without audio, with iCloud delivery latency. Screen control also needs Accessibility permission and the agent’s local control switch. Widget refresh timing is controlled by the system. Cleanup does not touch Documents, Photos, Downloads, Xcode archives or simulator device data. Selected cleanup data is permanently deleted; the app asks you to review the exact selection first.
