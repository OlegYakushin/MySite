A few practical things

Before you start.

Does Still predict illness?

No. It shows experimental comparisons with your own measurement history. It does not diagnose, predict disease or measure emotional stress from a watch.

Does reflection work on every phone?

Ready-made questions do. Apple AI requires a supported device, system settings and language. Russian is currently served with ready-made questions.

Where does my journal go?

It stays in the app on your devices and can sync between your paired phone and watch. We do not collect it on this website or use health data for advertising.

Can I download it now?

Still is being built. The App Store download link will appear here after the app is available. Screens shown here are clearly labeled examples.
