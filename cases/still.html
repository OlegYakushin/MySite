Personal context across iPhone and Watch.

A SwiftUI companion for personal health trends, a private journal, breathing pauses and recovery context. In development; not yet available on the App Store.

SwiftUIHealthKitwatchOSWidgetKit

The product

Bring available health measurements, personal check-ins and quiet daily routines into one place. Missing measurements stay missing instead of becoming invented scores.

Native engineering

HealthKit supplies the available measurements; WatchConnectivity carries journal changes and settings. Shared domain logic supports the phone, Watch and WidgetKit surfaces.

Privacy and resilience

Journal entries support local persistence, export, deletion and conflict handling. On-device reflection checks device and language availability, with ready-made questions as a fallback.

Engineering highlights

  • Health widgets are opt-in, and synthetic preview data is labeled.
  • Phone and Watch have separate storage containers and read their own HealthKit data.
  • Reduced Motion, large text and localization are part of the interface.

Onboarding captured from the working app with explicitly labeled synthetic example data.
