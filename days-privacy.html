Clear terms for sensitive travel data.

Travel history, passports, visas, and residence permits can be sensitive. Days is written to be direct about local storage, optional Apple services, and the choices available to you.

At a glance

  • Your travel and document records are saved locally.
  • Optional synchronization uses your private iCloud storage.
  • No app account, advertising or cross-app tracking. RevenueCat processes subscription data only.
  • Apple handles subscriptions and payment information.

This policy describes DAY’S 2.0.1, including photo trip import and RevenueCat subscription reporting. Earlier versions may not include these features.

1. Overview

DAY’S is operated by Olegotka Studio. It helps you record personal and business trips, organize travel documents and calculate stay days. The app requires no separate account or login. For privacy questions, contact hello@olegotka.es.

2. Information You Enter

You choose the information stored in DAY’S: citizenship and home country; trip countries and dates; business purpose, company, project and notes; passport, visa and residence-permit details, validity dates and stay quotas; and calculation preferences. The current app does not scan passports or upload document photographs. Language, appearance, reminder and synchronization preferences are also stored. The app does not request the current location of your device. With your photo-library permission, Premium photo import reads accessible photo dates and saved coordinates on the device to suggest trips. Country matching and review take place locally; photos and coordinates are not sent to RevenueCat or a developer-operated server. You review and edit suggestions before saving. A local import draft is separate from saved trips and can be deleted without deleting photographs.

3. Local Storage and Use

Your records are saved on your device. The app uses them to display your travel history, calculate stay days, show document expiry information and generate the summaries or forecasts you request. The core tracker works offline. We do not operate a server that receives your travel records or document details, and we cannot read those records from your device.

4. Optional iCloud Synchronization

Premium users may enable synchronization using their existing system iCloud account. When enabled, travel and document records are stored in the app’s iCloud container and synchronized through Apple services. This is your private iCloud storage; the developer has no access to its contents. Availability depends on your Apple account, iCloud settings and storage. Local saving continues when synchronization is unavailable. Turning synchronization off stops the app’s sync activity; it does not itself remove existing iCloud copies.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

Apple handles optional Premium subscriptions through the App Store and StoreKit. The app reads the product information and verified subscription status needed to enable Premium features. We do not receive your payment-card details. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. Apple processes purchase information under its own privacy policy. The RevenueCat integration reports purchase transaction data and a RevenueCat-generated anonymous subscriber identifier to synchronize subscriptions and understand subscription performance. RevenueCat does not receive your photographs, photo coordinates, travel history or travel-document records from this integration. Automatic device identifier collection is disabled. RevenueCat also processes technical subscription information such as app and SDK versions, storefront and currency, last-seen time, and a country derived from the transaction or connection IP address. RevenueCat states that it discards the IP address after determining the country. RevenueCat processes subscription information under its privacy policy.

6. Reminders, Analytics and Advertising

Limit reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. DAY’S does not use advertising SDKs or track you across other apps or websites. RevenueCat receives the subscription-related information described above; it does not receive in-app travel records for behavioral analytics. Apple may provide crash or usage diagnostics according to the system sharing choices you make; these are governed by Apple’s settings and policies.

7. Exports and Sharing

JSON backups and business-trip CSV reports are created only when you request them. You choose their destination through the system file or sharing interface. A backup or report can contain sensitive travel and document information, so select its destination carefully. Opening official country-rule sources or support links takes you to an external website whose own privacy policy applies. If you contact support, we receive the email and information you choose to send and use it to respond.

8. Retention and Your Controls

You can edit or delete individual records, export a backup and clear app records from Profile. The app also keeps recovery versions and, if enabled, iCloud snapshots; removing a current record does not guarantee immediate removal of every historical or exported copy. Manage iCloud app storage through your Apple settings and remove any exported backups separately. Device or iCloud backups may retain earlier data under your settings. There is no DAY’S account to delete. Cancelling Premium and deleting app data are separate actions. RevenueCat subscription records are separate from local travel records; clearing app records or uninstalling does not itself delete those subscription records. Contact our privacy address for questions or requests about subscription information.

9. Security and Children

DAY’S relies on local app storage and Apple platform protections. Protect your device, passcode and Apple account. No storage system is guaranteed to be completely secure. The app is not directed specifically at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an app account or a developer-operated backend.

10. Privacy Requests and Changes

Because we do not receive your in-app travel records, you control them on your device and in your Apple storage settings. Contact hello@olegotka.es for privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent to support. We will update this page and its date when privacy practices change. Apple services are covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.
