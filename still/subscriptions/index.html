Still Plus & subscription details

Still is in development. No subscription can be purchased on this website. Final prices and eligible offers will appear in the app/App Store before confirmation.

One Plus level, two periods

Monthly and yearly plans unlock the same Still Plus level. The monthly plan begins with a paid monthly period and has no free trial. The yearly plan offers a three-day free trial only for customers eligible for the configured annual introductory offer.

Before you confirm

  • The full yearly or monthly price appears in your App Store currency.
  • For an eligible annual trial, the app states three free days followed by the full annual charge and automatic yearly renewal.
  • Switching to monthly removes the trial action. Previous introductory offers in the subscription group may make you ineligible for another trial.
  • Apple AI reflection compatibility is disclosed separately. Plus is useful without Apple AI; ready-made questions are available when AI is unsupported.

Renewal and cancellation

The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled in your Apple account subscription settings before renewal. Cancel before the trial ends if you do not want the annual charge. Use Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or the management button in Still. Apple explains cancellation here.

Restore and refunds

Use Restore purchases in Still with the Apple account that purchased the subscription. Pending purchases need Apple's approval before access changes. Refunds and billing questions are handled by Apple; request a refund through Apple. Contact Still support for app/access problems without sending payment-card details.

Free features

Basic daily context, self-reported check-ins, ordinary journal entries, the one-minute breathing pause, basic widgets and privacy/export/deletion controls remain available without Plus. Longer displayed history and advanced overviews require Plus; unavailable records are never invented.
